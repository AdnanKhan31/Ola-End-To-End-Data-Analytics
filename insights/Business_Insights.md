# OLA Ride Analytics – Business Insights

## Project Overview

This project analyzes OLA ride booking data to understand booking trends, ride completion, cancellations, vehicle performance, revenue, and customer experience.

The analysis was performed using Microsoft Excel, PostgreSQL, SQL, and Power BI to transform raw ride booking data into meaningful business insights.

## Key Business Insights

### 1. Booking Trends

Daily and time-based booking analysis was performed to identify changes in ride demand across different dates, days of the week, and hours.

This helps identify high-demand and low-demand periods that can support better driver availability and resource planning.

### 2. Ride Completion & Location Performance

Pickup locations were analyzed based on total bookings and completed rides.

Comparing booking volume with successful ride completion helps identify locations where demand is high but ride fulfillment may require additional operational attention.

### 3. Cancellation Analysis

Customer and driver cancellations were analyzed separately to understand cancellation patterns and the reasons behind unsuccessful bookings.

This helps identify operational issues that may contribute to lost bookings and provides opportunities to improve the overall booking experience.

### 4. Vehicle Type Performance

Different vehicle types were compared using booking volume, cancellations, waiting times, customer ratings, and other performance indicators.

This analysis helps identify vehicle categories that perform strongly and those that may require closer monitoring.

### 5. Revenue & Booking Value

Booking values were analyzed across ride distances, vehicle types, payment methods, and pickup locations.

This helps understand revenue patterns and identify combinations associated with higher booking values.

### 6. Customer Experience

Customer ratings, driver ratings, VTAT (Vehicle Time of Arrival), and CTAT (Customer Time of Arrival) were analyzed to understand the relationship between operational performance and customer experience.

Longer waiting times and lower ratings can indicate areas where service quality may need improvement.

### 7. Booking Problems

Booking status was analyzed to identify the major types of unsuccessful bookings, including customer cancellations, driver cancellations, and incomplete rides.

Cancellation reasons were also analyzed to identify the most common problems affecting ride completion.

## Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis framework, the following areas can be considered for business improvement:

* Improve driver availability during high-demand periods.
* Monitor pickup locations with high demand but weaker ride completion.
* Analyze major customer and driver cancellation reasons and address recurring operational issues.
* Monitor vehicle types with higher cancellation rates or weaker customer ratings.
* Reduce waiting times where possible to improve customer experience.
* Monitor locations and time periods associated with lower ratings or higher unsuccessful rides.
* Use booking-value and ride-distance patterns to support revenue and pricing decisions.
* Continuously monitor booking status and cancellation trends through the Power BI dashboard.

## Tools Used

* Microsoft Excel
* PostgreSQL
* SQL
* Power BI

## Conclusion

The project demonstrates an end-to-end approach to data analytics, starting with data preparation and continuing through SQL-based business analysis and Power BI visualization.

The analysis provides a structured way to understand OLA ride demand, operational performance, cancellations, revenue, and customer experience, helping convert ride booking data into actionable business insights.
